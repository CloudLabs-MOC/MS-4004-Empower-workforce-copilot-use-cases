# Getting Started with Your MS-4004: Empower Your Workforce with 365 Copilot Use Cases
 
Welcome to your MS-4004: Empower Your Workforce with Microsoft 365 Copilot Use Cases workshop! We've prepared a seamless environment for you to explore and learn how Microsoft 365 Copilot can transform everyday work across Executive, Sales, IT, Marketing, Finance, HR, Operations, Communications, Customer Service, and Legal roles. Let's begin by making the most of this experience:

## Overview

In these hands-on labs, you will discover how Microsoft 365 Copilot embeds AI-powered assistance directly into the Microsoft 365 apps your workforce already uses every day, including Word, Excel, Outlook, Teams, PowerPoint, Planner, Loop, Whiteboard, and Viva Engage. Working through realistic, role-based scenarios, you will use Copilot Chat and Copilot in individual apps to synthesize communications, analyze data, draft content, and build project plans, and you will create and use custom Copilot agents that answer questions and deliver insights from organizational knowledge sources. Each module puts you in the shoes of a different business function: Executives synthesizing communications and business insights; Sales professionals researching prospects and building proposals; IT professionals planning projects and driving technology adoption; Marketing professionals researching markets and developing campaigns; Finance professionals analyzing data and evaluating contracts; HR professionals generating manager insights and building self-service agents; Operations professionals streamlining initiatives and managing expansions; Communications professionals driving engagement and developing content; Customer Service professionals improving service and resolving escalations; and Legal professionals managing risk and compliance. Along the way, you will also learn how to write effective Copilot prompts using the Goal, Context, Sources, and Expectations framework. By completing these labs, you will gain the practical experience needed to apply Microsoft 365 Copilot to real-world business scenarios and empower your workforce to work smarter, faster, and more collaboratively.

## Objectives

By the end of these labs, you will be able to:

1. **Empower Executives with Copilot:** Use Copilot in Teams, Word, Excel, and Planner to synthesize communications, create executive reports, analyze budgets, plan projects, and build and use a business insights agent.

2. **Empower Sales with Copilot:** Use a research agent and Copilot Chat to analyze markets and competitors, and use Copilot in Word, Excel, and Outlook to build sales proposals, response templates, and personalized customer emails, including creating and refining a proposal agent.

3. **Empower IT with Copilot:** Use Copilot Chat, Whiteboard, and PowerPoint to define project frameworks, identify risks, and build presentations, and use Viva Engage, Surveys, and Analyst agents to drive technology adoption and capture feedback.

4. **Empower Marketing with Copilot:** Create and use a market research agent, use an Analyst agent for recommendations, use Loop to build campaign plans, and use Word, Excel, and Idea Coach and Writing Coach agents to consolidate reports and develop campaign content.

5. **Empower Finance with Copilot:** Use Copilot in Excel, Teams, and Chat to analyze product lines, summarize meeting notes, evaluate potential acquisitions, model scenarios, assess vendor contracts, and use Loop and PowerPoint to turn insights into action.

6. **Empower HR with Copilot:** Use Copilot in Excel and Outlook to identify manager metrics, generate reports, and communicate findings, use Loop for collaborative planning, and create and use a self-service agent and a researcher agent for employee support.

7. **Empower Operations with Copilot:** Use Whiteboard, Word, and PowerPoint to brainstorm ideas and compare reports, use Loop to track milestones, create and use a facility expansion agent, and use OneNote and Outlook to update and communicate safety protocols.

8. **Empower Communications with Copilot:** Use Word to develop communication briefs, a researcher agent to identify trends, Excel to analyze communication metrics, PowerPoint for presentations, and Viva Engage, Copilot Chat, and coach/assistant agents to drive engagement.

9. **Empower Customer Service with Copilot:** Use Teams, Outlook, Word, OneNote, and Whiteboard to improve response quality, streamline ticket management, and maintain support content and team collaboration.

10. **Empower Legal with Copilot:** Use Word, Excel, Teams, and PowerPoint to draft and review legal documents, manage legal workflows, and accelerate research and compliance activities.

## Pre-requisites

- A basic understanding of Microsoft 365 applications such as Word, Excel, Outlook, Teams, PowerPoint, Planner, Loop, Whiteboard, and Viva Engage.

- Familiarity with the business processes relevant to each role-based module, such as sales, project management, financial reporting, HR operations, or communications planning.

- Basic knowledge of prompt-writing concepts, including how to structure a prompt using Goal, Context, Sources, and Expectations.


## Architecture

Across the course, these labs are built on a consistent two-exercise pattern: the first exercise has you use Copilot to **research, synthesize, or analyze** information relevant to that role, and the second has you use Copilot to **create, decide, or act** on it — often by building a custom agent that operationalizes the work for ongoing use. This "understand, then act" structure repeats for every business function covered, so what you practice compounds: by the end of the course, you have used nearly every Microsoft 365 Copilot surface (Word, Excel, Outlook, Teams, PowerPoint, Planner, Loop, Whiteboard, Viva Engage, Copilot Chat) and built or used a custom or prebuilt agent in almost every module.

1. **Communication Intelligence to Business Insight:** Synthesize updates, chats, and meetings across projects in Teams, then turn that information into executive reports, budget analysis, and project plans before building and using a Business Insights agent for on-demand answers.

2. **Market Research to Proposal Automation:** Use a Research agent and Copilot Chat to analyze markets and competitors, then produce a sales proposal, a personalized outreach email, a response template, and a data analysis before creating and refining a Proposal agent in Teams.

3. **Project Planning to Technology Adoption:** Define a project framework with Copilot Chat, identify risks in Whiteboard, and build a presentation, then drive adoption by identifying upcoming features, drafting a Viva Engage post, creating best-practice tips, and using Surveys and Analyst agents to capture and analyze feedback.

4. **Market Research to Campaign Development:** Create and use a Market Research agent, apply an Analyst agent's recommendations, and build a campaign plan in Loop, then consolidate reports, analyze a spreadsheet, and develop content with the Idea Coach and Writing Coach agents.

5. **Financial Analysis to Contract Action:** Analyze a product line, summarize meeting notes, evaluate a potential acquisition, and model scenarios, then assess vendor contracts, build a presentation, turn insights into actions, and draft a follow-up email.

6. **Manager Insights to Employee Self-Service:** Identify manager metrics and generate a report, and communicate findings, then collaboratively plan in Loop and create and use a Self-Service agent alongside a Researcher agent that gathers relocation and policy insights for employees.

7. **Initiative Streamlining to Expansion Management:** Brainstorm ideas in Whiteboard, compare reports, build a presentation, and prepare a vendor engagement, then track milestones in Loop, create and use a Facility Expansion agent, and update and email safety protocol changes.

8. **Trend Research to Engagement Content:** Develop a communication brief, use a Researcher agent to identify trends, analyze communication metrics, and build a presentation, then drive engagement with a Viva Engage announcement, a Copilot Chat meeting prep, and Idea Coach and Question Assistant agents.

9. **Service Mapping to Resolution Enhancement:** Map the support journey, prepare a support meeting, create a call template, and analyze data with an Analyst agent, then build a troubleshooting checklist, a response template, a guide with the Idea Coach agent, and an insights email.

10. **Litigation Preparation to Governance Reform:** Create legal guidance, summarize a vendor contract, prepare a brief with a Researcher agent, and build and query a Regulatory Assistant agent, then reform governance by updating company policy, visualizing risk areas, and building a presentation and notebook.

## Explanation of Components

1. **Copilot in Word:** Drafts, revises, and summarizes documents such as executive reports, sales proposals, response templates, communication briefs, and legal documents.

2. **Copilot in Excel:** Analyzes spreadsheet data to identify trends, model what-if scenarios, generate manager and financial reports, and surface actionable insights.

3. **Copilot in Outlook:** Drafts and refines professional, audience-appropriate emails, such as customer communications, findings summaries, and safety-change notifications.

4. **Copilot in Teams:** Synthesizes information from chats, meetings, and emails to keep users informed and to summarize meeting notes and cross-project updates.

5. **Copilot in PowerPoint:** Generates presentations and briefs from existing content, such as project risk decks, campaign presentations, and executive summaries.

6. **Copilot in Planner:** Builds and manages project plans, tracking milestones, dependencies, and progress for strategic and operational initiatives.

7. **Copilot in Loop:** Enables collaborative workspaces for campaign planning, milestone tracking, collaborative HR planning, and turning insights into action items.

8. **Copilot in Whiteboard:** Supports visual brainstorming sessions, such as identifying project risks or generating ideas for operational initiatives.

9. **Copilot in Viva Engage:** Drafts posts and announcements used to promote adoption, share best practices, and announce new custom agents to the organization.

10. **Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat:** A conversational, standalone Copilot experience used to research markets, identify trends, define frameworks, and gather information from web and organizational sources.

11. **Custom Copilot Agents:** No-code or low-code agents configured with specific instructions and knowledge sources so that users can get consistent, on-demand answers for a defined business scenario.

13. **OneDrive:** Cloud file storage that Copilot references when working with documents, spreadsheets, and presentations; files must be uploaded here before Copilot can use them.

14. **Copilot Prompting Framework:** A structured approach to writing prompts that is applied consistently across every module to produce clear, relevant, and actionable Copilot responses.
